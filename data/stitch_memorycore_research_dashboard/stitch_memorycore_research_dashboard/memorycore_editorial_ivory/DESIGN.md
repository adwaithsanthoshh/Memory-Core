## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, institutional research atmosphere combining the prestige of historical academic publishing with the precision of a modern scientific laboratory. It serves principal investigators, research fellows, and computational scientists who require dense, highly legible analytical interfaces without clinical sterile coldness.

The visual direction merges classical editorial gravitas with clean digital utility:
- **Tone & Atmosphere:** Scholarly, authoritative, luminous, and contemplative. Generous warm space replaces utilitarian starkness with archival depth.
- **Visual Movements:** Traditional Editorial Typography meets Modern Analytical Minimalism. The design rejects glowing neon motifs and excessive glassmorphic effects in favor of tactile off-white paper tones, hairline structural dividers, and deep garnet ink accents.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires rigor, patience, and unhurried intellectual clarity. Every interactive component behaves with deliberate poise, reminiscent of an archival folio or premium leather-bound compendium translated into digital precision.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from vintage parchment, archival buckram bindings, and rich iron-gall inks. High readability is preserved through calibrated contrast ratios adhering strictly to WCAG AAA for key editorial reading experiences.

### Primary Accents
- **Deep Garnet (`#650004`):** The primary color and visual anchor. Employed for high-priority interactive triggers, active navigation markers, key analytical matrix tags, and institutional emphasis.
- **Soft Rose / Dusty Blush (`#DF8F9C`):** The secondary accent. Used sparingly for quiet status tags, subtle reference lines in charts, soft focused outlines, and non-destructive badges.

### Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base (`#F9F6EE`):** Warm editorial ivory parchment base providing an organic, glare-free working canvas.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFDF6`):** Clean, illuminated card surfaces and elevated content modules.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#F4EEDC`):** Recessed containers, side rails, table headers, and secondary paneling.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#EDE6D2`):** Inset controls, active item highlights, and segmented pill backgrounds.

### Typography & Structure
- **Primary Ink (`#1E1A1B`):** Deep soot-black for display headings and body copy requiring maximum legibility.
- **Secondary Ink (`#574B4E`):** Warm iron-gray for meta-annotations, table captions, and supporting copy.
- **Muted Ink (`#7E7275`):** Soft charcoal for disabled states, structural rules, and peripheral metric labels.
- **Delicate Rule / Border (`#DFD6C2`):** Hairline dividing lines, panel perimeters, and card boundaries.
- **Subtle Rule / Hover Border (`#D8CEB6`):** Elevated boundary states and active field outlines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances literary prestige and mathematical precision through a tripartite font distribution:

- **Editorial Serifs (`Newsreader`):** Applied exclusively to document titles, section headers, and callout statements. It introduces scholarly authority, optical warmth, and comfortable long-form cadence. It should be typeset with slight negative letter tracking at display scales.
- **Instrumental Sans (`Inter`):** The primary engine for body prose, multi-column research records, dialogues, and form entries. Clean, tall x-height, and neutral geometry guarantee readability across intensive data views.
- **Scientific Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to telemetry values, raw chemical nomenclature, timestamp arrays, status micro-chips, and dataset parameters. It brings clinical structure to numerical readouts and alignment clarity to tabulations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored by a structured 12-column analytical grid echoing classic broadsheet and journal column layouts, moving dynamically into modular panels for instrumentation and observation matrices.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, 24px (`gutter-lg`) gutters, 48px (`margin-lg`) canvas margins. Sidebars for indexing and reference parameters remain docked at a fixed 280px width, while analytical viewports expand fluidly.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 24px (`gutter`) gutters, 32px (`margin`) outer margins. Auxiliary scientific inspectors convert into collapsible side drawers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`gutter-sm`) gutters, 16px (`margin-sm`) margins. Multi-column data sets fold linearly; primary action triggers anchor to standard sticky bottom ribbons.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Spatial progression follows an intentional base-4 cadence (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`). Micro-components like table rows and badge internals leverage dense vertical padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to maximize visible research data per viewport, whereas macro-sections and document boundaries use generous intervals (`space-xl`) to preserve academic breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the physical weight of an archival paper volume, this design system repudiates heavy dropshadows, blurred ambient glows, or glassy light-scattering. Visual layer distinction relies on **tonal stacking** and **crisp mechanical hairline borders**.

### Hierarchy of Planes
- **Ground (Canvas):** The baseline ambient backdrop (`#F9F6EE`). Unbordered, calm, and neutral.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Elevated directly above the ground using `#FFFDF6` and defined by a strict 1px solid hairline (`#DFD6C2`). No shadow is cast; edge clarity determines boundary.
- **Level 2 (Active Inspections & Floating Popovers):** Panels requiring direct focus utilize surface `#FFFDF6`, bordered with `#D8CEB6`, accompanied by an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 26, 27, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 26, 27, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogues & Systematic Overlays):** Modals sit atop an ivory-tinted semi-translucent scrim (`rgba(30, 26, 27, 0.4)`). Containers are bounded by a dual-stroke impression: a 1px border of `#D8CEB6` backed by an ambient physical drop of `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 26, 27, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape grammar adheres strictly to subtle, measured corner treatments (`roundedness: 1`), reinforcing precision, institutional discipline, and editorial print binding.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Cards):** Built with a discreet radius of `0.25rem` (4px). Corners appear crisp and intentional, avoiding the casual informality of circular forms.
- **Large Containers & Modal Frames:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`) to maintain structural softness without diluting geometric rigor.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Pure pill radii are reserved exclusively for compact numeric status badges, analytical data counters, and live recording chips to create immediate visual distinction against rectangular data tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Garnet (`#650004`) background, text in Ivory (`#FAF4D4`), font set to `Inter` (500 weight, 14px), radius `0.25rem`. Hover state shifts to `#4E0003` with a crisp 1px highlight line. Focus ring uses a 2px offset border in `#DF8F9C`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background of `#FFFDF6`, 1px border in `#DFD6C2`, text in Primary Ink (`#1E1A1B`). On hover, background shifts to `#F4EEDC` and border darkens to `#D8CEB6`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** No border or background fill. Ink transitions from `#574B4E` to `#1E1A1B` with an underline accent on hover.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Analytical Chips:** Rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (11px, uppercase). Background in `#F4EEDC`, bounded by a 1px hairline of `#DFD6C2`, text in `#574B4E`.
- **Status Badges:** Critical / Active status utilizes `#650004` background with `#FAF4D4` glyphs; auxiliary and review states use `#DF8F9C` at 20% opacity with `#650004` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Constructed with `#FFFDF6` field background, 1px border of `#DFD6C2`, and 4px radius. Typography is `Inter` 14px in `#1E1A1B`. Placeholder copy in `#7E7275`.
- **Active Focus:** Focus shifts border color immediately to `#650004` with a gentle 1px outer glow ring in `#DF8F9C` (40% opacity). No layout shifts.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Selection Controls:** 16px square (or circular) boxes with 1px border in `#D8CEB6`, surface in `#FFFDF6`.
- **Checked State:** Fill turns solid `#650004` with an Ivory checkmark or inner dot.

### Lists & Data Matrices
- **Scientific Tables:** Clean row architecture with zero vertical interior gridlines. Rows alternate cleanly or separate via 1px border bottoms in `#DFD6C2`.
- **Row Heights:** Compact 36px or comfortable 48px options. Column headers are styled in `JetBrains Mono` (11px uppercase), `#574B4E`, with bottom borders in `#D8CEB6`.
- **Hover Rows:** Subtle highlight transition to `#F4EEDC` at 50% opacity.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFDF6`, 1px border `#DFD6C2`, padding `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px). Header sections feature a `Newsreader` (20px, semi-bold) title paired with a right-aligned monospace metric indicator.
- **Divider Treatment:** Card interior separators employ single-pixel solid rules of `#DFD6C2` without gradients.

### Specialized Lab Components
- **Specimen / Parameter Readout:** Key-value pairs displayed with key in `Inter` (12px, `#574B4E`) and value in `JetBrains Mono` (14px bold, `#1E1A1B`).
- **Archival Notes Drawer:** Lateral slide-out panel utilizing `#FFFDF6` surface with Newsreader body copy, designed specifically for qualitative peer observations and experimental notations.
